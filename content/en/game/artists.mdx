## Verified studio-level attribution

The official Steam listing credits KotaMota Games as the developer and describes the collectible cards as hand-drawn. It does not assign the card illustrations, character art, or art direction to a named person.

Individual artist names are not established by the supplied sources. KotaMota Games is the supported studio-level credit, but a studio credit must not be converted into the name of an assumed individual artist.

## Where to verify individual names

Use the in-game credits as the authoritative source for individual names and roles. Check the full release's Credits screen for entries such as illustrator, card artist, character artist, or art director. Record each role with its exact displayed name before adding a person-level attribution.

Until that evidence is supplied, this page will not invent a person, infer a name from a username, or repeat an unanswered community guess.

## Attribution is not reuse permission

A credit identifies authorship or production involvement. It does not grant permission to copy, redistribute, or republish game art. Do not extract or distribute paid Supporter Pack images, card illustrations, character artwork, logos, or other protected assets without permission from the relevant rights holder.

The [current cards reference](/en/cards) lists community-documented card names without reproducing the protected illustrations.
