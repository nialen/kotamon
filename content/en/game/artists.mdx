## Who created the KOTAMON card artwork?

The official Steam listing credits KotaMota Games as the developer and describes dozens of collectible cards with hand-drawn illustrations. Publicly available official sources currently do not provide a complete individual artist list. This page therefore separates the verified studio credit from person-level roles that still need authoritative evidence.

For the release identity, platform details and related official references, return to [Game Information](/en/game). You can also open the [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4294490/KOTAMON/) that supports the statements above.

## Who are the KOTAMON card artists?

The public store material does not assign the card illustrations, character art or art direction to named people. KotaMota Games is the supported studio-level credit, but a studio credit must not be converted into the name of an assumed individual card artist.

The [current cards reference](/en/cards) documents card names and collection groupings without claiming that the list identifies their artists. For other item categories beyond cards, use the [Collectibles Hub](/en/collectibles).

## What we know about the KOTAMON artwork

The official description establishes that hand-drawn card artwork is part of the game. It does not establish which illustrator created a particular card, whether one artist or several artists divided the set, or who handled character art and art direction.

That distinction matters: the existence of illustrations is verified, while individual authorship remains incomplete in the public evidence reviewed for this guide.

## Are individual KOTAMON illustrators credited?

Currently available public official sources do not provide a complete list of individual illustrators. That does not mean no artist exists or that no illustrator is credited anywhere; it means this guide does not yet have reliable evidence for a complete person-level list.

## How to verify KOTAMON artwork credits

Use the full release's in-game Credits screen as the next authoritative place to check individual names and roles. Look for entries such as illustrator, card artist, character artist or art director, then record each role with its exact displayed name before adding an attribution.

Until that evidence is supplied, this page will not invent a person, infer a name from a username or repeat an unanswered community guess. If a future official credit, patch announcement or Steam page names an individual, the attribution can be updated with that source.

## Attribution is not reuse permission

A credit identifies authorship or production involvement. It does not grant permission to copy, redistribute or republish game art. Do not extract or distribute paid Supporter Pack images, card illustrations, character artwork, logos or other protected assets without permission from the relevant rights holder.
