## What the official listing confirms

- **Developer and publisher:** KotaMota Games and Polnoch.
- **Store identity:** Steam App ID 4294490.
- **Platform and play mode:** Windows PC, single-player.
- **Store features:** Steam achievements and Steam Cloud.
- **Language options:** the listing includes English and Simplified Chinese among its interface and subtitle languages. Check the store language table for your preferred language; do not infer full voice support.

The [official Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4294490/KOTAMON/) is the purchase and availability reference.
