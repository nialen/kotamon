## How to read the CPU and GPU labels

The processor entries name the Core i3 and Core i7 families without a specific generation or model. That makes a simple “i7 is enough” conclusion unreliable: compare your actual processor, not only the family name. The listing also permits equivalent hardware but does not supply an AMD model-by-model equivalence chart.

The minimum graphics entry explicitly includes 4 GB of video memory. That is distinct from the 4 GB system RAM entry. This page does not treat integrated graphics, a similarly named laptop GPU or a shared-memory figure as automatically equivalent to the listed card.

## Storage and platform boundaries

Steam lists 3 GB available space for minimum and 5 GB for recommended. These are the published figures, not measured download sizes or guarantees about future patches and add-ons. Check free disk space as well as installed RAM before starting an installation.

The supplied requirements describe Windows PC. They do not establish native macOS or Linux support, a console edition, or a verified Steam Deck performance profile. Avoid treating an unofficial compatibility report as a platform announcement.
