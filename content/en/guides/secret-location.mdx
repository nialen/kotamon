## Where is the KOTAMON easter egg?

The official GIGA patch confirms one hidden surprise for players who reach a secret destination. Community reports associate the hidden area with Jenny's house and describe an entry near the garage doorframe. The official announcement does not publish exact coordinates or the collision-based steps used by the community.

This guide currently covers the confirmed hidden surprise and the related community-reported secret location. It does not turn an unofficial route into an official mechanic or claim that every game build uses the same entry method.

**Spoiler warning:** The route is optional, uses collision behavior and may reveal an easter egg before you find it naturally. Save first and open the checklist only when you are ready.

This exploration guide belongs to the [Collectibles Hub](/en/collectibles), but the evidence does not establish that the secret is required for every collection goal.

## How to get inside the house

The community-reported entry is by Jenny's house, near the garage doorframe; this guide does not verify a normal way to enter the house interior. Save first, because the current route relies on collision behavior and may not work in every patch.

## How to reach the upstairs secret area

Current evidence does not establish a separately accessible upstairs area. Players use “upstairs” and “inside the house” to describe the same community-reported hidden-area route, so this page keeps that route together instead of treating it as another secret.

## Where is the KOTAMON secret room?

The official patch calls it a hidden surprise, while community reports place the related secret location by Jenny's house. The sources do not identify a distinct, officially named secret room, so do not assume a separate room or entrance beyond the route documented here.

## How to reach the hidden area

Current community reports use the garage doorframe and a beer can to interact with collision and reach the hidden space. This is not documented as a normal movement mechanic, and small positioning differences can make it fail.

## Common mistakes when looking for the secret

Because the route relies on collision, a future update can move, close, or otherwise change the entry method. A failed attempt does not prove the official surprise was removed, and a successful attempt does not prove the same route will work in another build.

Do not use coordinate editors, trainers, or other third-party tools to force entry. Do not claim a fixed reward, since the supplied sources do not establish one. Check [KOTAMON Updates & Patch Notes](/en/updates) if a newer official announcement may have changed the route.

If an attempt affects saving or loading, stop and follow the [save not working guide](/en/guides/save-not-working). For the normal exploration loop, return to the [gameplay guide](/en/guides/gameplay).
