**Spoiler warning:** This page explains how players report reaching the hidden area associated with Jenny's house. The route is optional, uses collision behavior, and may reveal an easter egg before you find it naturally.

This optional exploration guide belongs to the [Collectibles Hub](/en/collectibles), alongside figurines and audiotapes.

## What the official hint confirms

The official GIGA patch says a surprise was added for players who reach the secret destination. That confirms the hidden discovery, but the announcement does not publish a step-by-step route, exact coordinates, or a guaranteed reward.

The official hint and the route below are different evidence. The destination comes from a developer announcement. The entry method comes from community demonstrations summarized in the saved research.

## Community-reported route

Current community reports use the garage doorframe and a beer can to interact with collision and reach the hidden space. This is not documented as a normal movement mechanic, and small positioning differences can make it fail.

## Patch warning and safe limits

Because the route relies on collision, a future update can move, close, or otherwise change the entry method. A failed attempt does not prove the official surprise was removed, and a successful attempt does not prove the same route will work in another build.

Do not use coordinate editors, trainers, or other third-party tools to force entry. Do not claim a fixed reward, since the supplied sources do not establish one.

If an attempt affects saving or loading, stop and follow the [save not working guide](/en/guides/save-not-working). For the normal exploration loop, return to the [gameplay guide](/en/guides/gameplay).
