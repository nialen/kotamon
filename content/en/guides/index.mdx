## Working toward completion?

Keep the [Cards Hub](/en/cards) beside your collection and use the [achievements reference](/en/achievements) to check milestones. A money target, a complete color set and a foil collection are different goals; the relevant guides explain those boundaries instead of promising a single route that completes everything.
