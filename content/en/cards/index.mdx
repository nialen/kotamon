## How to read the evidence labels

The color-set completion structure comes from Steam's public achievements. The 40 individual base-card names come from the maintained community checklist, so each is labeled `Community name`. Kotalion is the one card named directly by the public Steam achievement list.

Exact HP, ATK, condition values, prices, and foil eligibility are intentionally omitted. The supplied official evidence does not publish a complete per-card stat catalog, and condition-dependent prices should not be treated as fixed card data.

## Collection boundaries

Do not turn empty binder slots into invented cards. The reviewed community evidence supports 40 base cards across four color sets plus Kotalion as a separate special card. It does not establish additional obtainable special cards.

Track foil progress separately from this base-card checklist. Steam publishes dedicated foil achievements, but the saved official sources do not provide a complete card-by-card foil list or a guaranteed acquisition rule. Use the [foil cards guide](/en/guides/foil-cards) and [achievement reference](/en/achievements) for those distinct goals.
