**Spoiler warning:** This page explains the current audiotape collection loop and the reported 6 of 7 problem. Keep the route checklist closed if you want to find the tapes without guidance.

## Achievement requirements

Steam publishes audiotape milestones for finding 1, 5, and 7 tapes. The final requirement is seven valid counts. Steam does not publish seven fixed locations, a coordinate list, or a requirement to find an eighth physical tape.

Treat the unlock notification and recorded count as the achievement evidence. A visible tape model and a counted pickup are not proven to be interchangeable in every affected save.

## Current issue reports

The maintained community source records players reaching a displayed 6 of 7 state or seeing the physical display and achievement count disagree. Some players report that another physical pickup caused the final unlock, but that is an issue report, not an official requirement, a universal workaround, or proof that the achievement requires eight tapes.

Do not delete a save or overwrite the only good slot to test that report. Preserve a separate manual save and record what the display, counter, and Steam notification show after each pickup.
