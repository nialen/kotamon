**Spoiler warning:** The checklist below reveals the current collection pattern and the order in which to search. Keep it closed if you want to discover the figurines without route guidance.

For other discoveries, use the [Collectibles Hub](/en/collectibles). The [dated update summary](/en/updates#august-22-2026) explains the official change to figurine appearance chances without inventing a drop rate.

## What counts as a complete route

Current guides agree on a mixed pattern: six checks are tied to documented landmarks, while the remaining discoveries come from trash piles. This means the collection is not twelve fixed map points, and it should not be treated as a twelve-pin coordinate map.

The specific figurine identity at a landmark can differ between players. Track the total collected count, not an assumed identity-to-location pairing. Steam publishes milestones at 1, 5, 9, and 12 figurines.

## Patch-sensitive limits

The August 22 developer update adjusted figurine appearance chances. That supports repeating normal trash-pile cycles, but it does not establish a fixed drop rate or a guaranteed number of piles per figurine.

If a later update changes spawn behavior, compare the game date and current guide evidence before repeating the route. Do not use coordinate editors, save edits, or invented drop-rate formulas.

For the cumulative unlock list, use the [achievement guide](/en/achievements). If collection progress or upgrades appear to roll back, stop and follow the [save troubleshooting guide](/en/guides/save-not-working).
