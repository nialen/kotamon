## Use milestones to track progress

Steam lists figurine milestones at 1, 5, 9 and 12, and audiotape milestones at 1, 5 and 7. The [Achievements reference](/en/achievements) collects those targets alongside the game's other unlocks. These totals are progress checks, not a fixed coordinate map or a guarantee about the next trash pile.

For figurines, the existing community route describes six landmark checks followed by random discoveries. For tapes, the available reports describe trash-pile finds and occasional counter discrepancies. Record your starting count and compare it after a pickup; do not replace a missing item with an invented location.

## Patch changes and safe exploration

The August 22 announcement increased figurine appearance chances but did not publish a numerical drop rate. The [Updates page](/en/updates) explains the relevant changes so you can compare an older walkthrough with the dated official notes.

Before investigating a counter mismatch or trying the secret route, preserve a good save. If progress rolls back, use the [save troubleshooting guide](/en/guides/save-not-working) instead of deleting or editing save files. The secret-area route is a community report, not an official movement tutorial or a guaranteed reward route.

For the normal yard cycle, return to [Gameplay](/en/guides/gameplay). If your next goal is card condition, repair or foil collection, use the [Cards Hub](/en/cards); it groups those tasks separately from exploration.
